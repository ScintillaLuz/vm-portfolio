## Overview

MasterClass had grown its content catalogue significantly but the mobile navigation hadn't kept pace. Users were struggling to find content relevant to them.

## Problem

Discovery on mobile was buried under a navigation pattern that made sense on desktop but failed users on smaller screens. Drop-off rates on the browse experience were significantly higher on mobile than desktop.

## Process

We started with a two-week research sprint, running usability sessions with existing and lapsed subscribers. The patterns were clear: users wanted to browse by mood and topic, not by instructor name.

## Outcome

The redesigned experience shipped to 100% of mobile users in Q2 2024. Browse engagement increased and time-to-first-lesson dropped measurably.
