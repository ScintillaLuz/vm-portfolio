## Testing this

Lying is a human activity, the AI is just a powerful prediction model. It gives you the wrong answer not because of an intention. It doesn't think nor the humans creating the model can inject their thinking since the answer is creating the moment the prompt is sent to it. However, it is programmed to give you the most probable answer no matter what, which leads to fabricating an answer when it doesn't have one. When telling the AI "you forgot this" or "this was wrong" it usually tries to correct itself, leading to think it had knowledge of being wrong, but it doesn't because to know means to think and have consciousness and the AI has no consciousness. The farce is dressing up a machine like a person, and wanting us to converse to machines as if they were people, because of the benefit of smoother, easier interaction. The reality is that the mask the chatbot is wearing obfuscates what's behind, the program.
